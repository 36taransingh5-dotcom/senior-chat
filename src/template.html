Fair questions

Is it free?

Joining the waitlist is free, and so is your first chat at launch. After that, conversations are priced per chat — never a commission on where you enrol.

How are seniors verified?

Three steps: an active university email, proof of current enrolment, and a short video check. If any of the three lapse, the profile comes down.

Which destinations can I ask about?

Any country, any university. Seniors join as they verify, so coverage grows every week — tell us where you're headed and we'll match you as soon as someone's there.

What can I actually ask?

Anything a counsellor can't or won't answer — real rent, real weather, making friends, safety, part-time work, whether the course is worth the fees.

We're letting students in from the waitlist, in order. The earlier you join, the earlier you talk to someone who is already there.
