Why we're building this

Fair questions

Is it free?

Free during early access. The first 500 on the waitlist get priority matching and locked-in free chats at launch.

How are seniors verified?

Three steps: an active university email, proof of current enrolment, and a short video check with our team. If any of the three lapse, the profile comes down.

Which destinations can I ask about?

The UK and Canada at launch — Toronto, Vancouver, London, Manchester and more. The US and Australia open next, in waitlist order.

What can I actually ask?

Anything a counsellor can't or won't answer — real rent, real weather, making friends, safety, part-time work, whether the course is worth the fees. Nothing is off-limits except the seniors' own privacy.

Be first in line.

We're letting students in from the waitlist, in order. The earlier you join, the earlier you talk to someone who is already there.
